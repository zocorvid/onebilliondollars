Welcome to the Official Website of Carmilla

Carmilla is a fast-paced VA deathrock band based on the 1872 sapphic vampire novella. In our music we are inspired by gothic literature, dark romanticism, and the classic sounds of Batcave deathrock and gothic rock...accompanied by surf rock, and gothabilly, as well!
